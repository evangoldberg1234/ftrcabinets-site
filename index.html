Design help

We help design the kitchens — not just ship boxes.

Upfront layout work so cabinets fit dirty walls and off-dimension rooms without field drama.

  • Plan for the half-inch. In multifamily, 20 apartments can each be off by half an inch. We design boxes and fillers so cabinets still land clean — less field scribing, fewer on-site fixes.
  • Layouts that absorb error. Flexible cabinet runs (including planned fillers) so the boxes eat drywall and layout mistakes instead of the GC re-framing or field-modifying cabinets.
  • Blind corners with room to adjust. On U-shaped kitchens we use blind corner cabinets (about a BL39–BL42 range) that give roughly 3 inches of play when walls come in off-dimension.
  • Counters designed for real installs. We know the crew will trim tops on site — that's expected — and we still design around the problem corners and reveals upfront.
  • Easier for the GC and developer. Fewer RFIs and change orders, faster install, and a consistent finished look across every unit.

FAQ

Questions GCs and developers ask

Straight answers on supply-only multifamily cabinets and countertops.

Do you install the cabinets?

No. FTR Kitchens is supply only. We deliver labeled packages to your site; your GC or install crew puts them in — and you keep that margin.

What areas do you serve?

Multifamily projects across Connecticut, New York (including the NYC metro), New Jersey, and Pennsylvania — markets like Hartford, Stamford, Newark, and Philadelphia.

What is the minimum project size?

We focus on multifamily. Typical jobs are 40+ units with repeating kitchen types so factory pricing stays sharp.

Do you supply countertops?

Yes. Quartz and stone countertops are part of the package, sized from your unit drawings and delivered with the cabinets.

Can you handle ADA / accessible units?

Yes. We build cabinet packages to the project’s accessible-unit specs so ADA kitchens match the rest of the building.

How do I get a quote?

Send unit plans and a finish schedule for a free takeoff. Pricing is open book — you see the supplier cost and our markup. Call (203) 580-9994 or use the quote form on this page.

Do you help with kitchen design?

Yes. We help design layouts upfront — fillers, blind corners with adjustment, and counter reveals — so cabinets fit off-dimension rooms with fewer field fixes and change orders.
